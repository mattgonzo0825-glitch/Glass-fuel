Manual DTE calibration: the full-tank baseline scales with the average MPG you enter: about 380 miles at 17 MPG up to about 440 miles at 25 MPG. This never affects Live OBD data.

iPad connection
  1. Open the hosted HTTPS page in Bluefy – Web BLE Browser. Safari does not expose this BLE connection.
  2. Plug in Veepeak, switch ignition on, then tap Connect and choose VEEPEAK.
  3. Manual DTE mode works without Bluetooth. Enter the DTE shown on your dash and the mileage shown in Maps.

The page does not read Apple Maps routes directly. GPS only subtracts distance you actually travel while this page remains active.
